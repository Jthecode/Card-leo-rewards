Referral Rewards

Every Approved Referral Adds To Your Tower.

Members get their own referral link. When someone joins, pays, and becomes approved, that referral can count toward rewards and leaderboard placement.

$7 Direct Referral Reward

Member A earns $7 when Member A directly refers an approved paid member, like Member A referring Member B or Member C.

$2 Team-Building Reward

When Member B refers Member C and Member C becomes approved, it can count as a $2 team-building reward example.

10 Approved Referrals

Once Member A reaches 10 approved direct referrals, Member A can qualify for the $100 allowance.

Membership Rules

Clear Pricing. Clear Approval. Clear Payouts.

These rules keep the member experience direct and easy to understand.

  • $25 one-time activation fee.
  • $20/month recurring membership.
  • Recurring billing happens on the 10th.
  • Approval happens automatically after payment.
  • Earn $7 per approved direct referral.
  • Team-building example: Member B → Member C = $2.
  • 10 approved referrals can qualify for a $100 allowance.
  • Payouts are sent between the 1st and 3rd.
  • Existing member tiers stay the same.
  • Denied, cancelled, or unpaid members should not count as approved.
