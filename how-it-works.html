Referral Link Guide

How To Find, Copy, And Send Your Referral Link

Your referral link is one of the most important tools in your Card Leo Rewards member portal. Always send your personal link when inviting someone so the system can connect that recruit back to you.

Follow the steps on this page from top to bottom when recruiting someone new.

1

Sign In To Your Member Portal

Go to the Card Leo Rewards Member Login page and sign in using the email address and password connected to your active membership.

  • Open cardleorewards.com/login.html .
  • Enter your member email and password.
  • After login, open your main member dashboard.
2

Find Your Referral Link

Look for the referral section inside your dashboard. Your personal referral URL should appear there.

Example

Your referral link will look similar to: https://www.cardleorewards.com/signup.html?ref=YOUR-CODE

  • Do not remove the referral code from the end of the link.
  • Do not send only the main Card Leo Rewards homepage when you want the person connected to you.
  • Use your personal referral URL whenever possible.
3

Copy Your Referral Link

Use the Copy Referral Link button in your member portal. Once copied, the complete link is stored on your phone or computer clipboard.

  • Press Copy Referral Link .
  • Wait for the portal to confirm that the link was copied.
  • Send where you want to send it.
  • Press and hold inside the message area on mobile and choose Paste , or use your computer's paste command.
4

Send The Link To Your Recruit

You can send the same personal referral link through several different channels. Use the communication method that is easiest for the person you are inviting.

Text Message Send By SMS

Open your phone's Messages app, select the person, paste your referral link, add a short explanation, and send.

Email Send By Email

Create a new email, explain Card Leo Rewards briefly, paste the referral link, and ask the recruit to use that link when creating the account.

Social Media Instagram / Facebook

Paste the link into a direct message. Tell the recruit to open your exact link rather than searching for the signup page separately.

Share Button Phone Share Menu

If your portal displays a Share button, use it to open your phone's share menu and choose Messages, Mail, or another supported app.

5

Tell The Recruit What To Do

Sending the link is only the first part. Make sure the recruit understands the signup and membership process so the referral does not remain incomplete.

  • Open the referral link you sent.
  • Complete the Card Leo Rewards signup form.
  • Create their account using their own email address.
  • Continue to secure membership.
  • Complete the required membership.
  • Once membership is confirmed and the account is approved, the referral can move into the active/approved stage.
6

Track The Recruit In Your Portal

Return to your member portal after sending your referral. Your referral tools are designed to show the difference between a recruit who has started and a recruit who has completed activation.

  • Check Pending Referrals for recruits who have not completed all requirements.
  • Check Approved Referrals for qualifying active members.
  • Review your referral tower to understand direct and team activity.
  • Review rewards and allowance progress separately from pending recruits.

Membership Rules

Key Rules Members Should Remember

These are the important rules to understand when tracking membership status, referrals, and allowances.

  • Your membership is a recurring $20 monthly membership .
  • Recurring billing happens on the 10th .
  • Approval happens automatically after payment is confirmed .
  • Earn $7 when Member A referred Member B and Member B becomes active and approved.
  • Team-building example: Member B referred Member C = $1 for Member A .
  • Allowances are dispersed on the 1st and 3rd after the first 30 Days. of every month.
  • Existing tier allowances are released after Member A recruits 4 new active team members .
  • Tiers stay the same or increase with active members.
  • Denied, cancelled, or unpaid members will not count as approved referrals.
