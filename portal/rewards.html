How Your Reward Money Moves

Each number on this page represents a different stage. Earnings are not considered card-loaded allowance until they actually reach the card-loading stage.

Pending Earnings

Referral activity has been recorded but is still waiting for qualification or approval.

2

Approved Allowance

Qualified rewards have been approved and entered into your allowance ledger.

3

Available To Load

Approved allowance is ready for the Card Leo card-loading process.

4

Loaded To Card

Allowance has been successfully transferred to your Card Leo allowance card.
